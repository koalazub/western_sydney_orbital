Click or focus the canvas to use the in-game controls. Left drag orbits, right drag pans, and the wheel zooms. Keys 1 through 5 select unrestricted debug views: orbit, first person, third person, free flight and overview. Keys 6 and 7 show the fixed top and bottom ship sensors at 512 by 512 pixels with 8 bits per RGB channel and fixed exposure. WASD returns to the debug camera and moves it; Q and E move vertically, and Shift increases speed. F frames the selected object. Space runs or pauses the mission, period steps, T changes playback rate, and R opens reset confirmation. I opens the entity inspector, H opens help, M maximizes and X toggles fullscreen. Tab navigates game buttons, and Enter activates them. Escape releases the mouse and canvas focus so Tab can leave the game. Camera movement does not control the lander.
