Click or focus the canvas to use the in-game controls. Space runs or pauses the mission. While running, WASD applies ship thrust: W and S forward and back, A and D left and right. E applies up thrust to lift off from the pad; Q applies down thrust. Manual thrust turns off the example controller. Releasing keys stops thrust, not the ship's velocity. Hold Alt, called Option on macOS, with WASD or Q and E to move only the debug camera. Shift boosts debug movement only. Left drag orbits, right drag pans, and the wheel or trackpad pinch zooms the debug view. Keys 1 through 5 select debug views: orbit, first person, third person, free flight and overview. Keys 6 and 7 show the top and bottom ship sensors at 512 by 512 pixels with 8 bits per RGB channel and fixed exposure. Debug movement, pinch and wheel input do not change the ship sensor mounts or optics. F frames the selected object. Period steps, T changes playback rate, and R opens reset confirmation. I opens the entity inspector and H opens help. M maximizes within the browser window. X or the Fullscreen button enters actual browser fullscreen; Escape exits fullscreen. Tab navigates game buttons, and Enter activates them. Escape releases the mouse and canvas focus so Tab can leave the game. The advisory path preview does not control the ship.
